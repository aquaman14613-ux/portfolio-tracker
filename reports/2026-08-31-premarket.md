### NVO — Novo Nordisk · 7.90% · −0.48% vs cost · −1.34% today — SELL-SIDE SPLIT
Largest single-stock holding, and the only name where two major banks moved in opposite directions inside 48 hours. Aug 25: shares +2.2% as JPMorgan raised 2026 sales estimates and price target, with ex-US Wegovy growth beating. Aug 27: China's NMPA accepted the Wegovy marketing application [A]. Also Aug 27: **Deutsche Bank downgraded to Sell** [B]. Interim 2026 dividend of DKK 3.75/share paid in August [A].

Competitively, Lilly's orforglipron (Foundayo) is FDA-approved and shipping — the first oral small-molecule GLP-1, taken any time of day with no food or water restriction, and it beats oral semaglutide on both A1C and weight loss. *Thesis:* China acceptance is a genuine long-duration positive on the largest untapped obesity market. Orforglipron is a structural threat precisely in the convenience segment Novo's oral franchise was meant to own. At 7.9% this is the biggest idiosyncratic single-name risk, ~2.1pts below the ceiling, and the analyst community is openly split on it.
