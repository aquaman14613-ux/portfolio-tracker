AVGO · Broadcom — Material, event risk

Thesis read: The custom-ASIC franchise is the thesis and it is being actively contested. VRT · Vertiv — Material, negative overhang

Stock is ~30% off its $376 high, sell-side is 24 buys / 0 sells with a $338 mean target, and the position is −3.95% from cost — well inside the −15% review trigger, but this is the holding where the gap between analyst enthusiasm and the actual disclosure record is widest.

NVO · Novo Nordisk — Material, negative

Deutsche Bank downgraded to Sell from Hold on 27 August, cutting its target 9% to DKK 265 from DKK 290 — the first Sell from a major bank in months, taking the distribution to 5 buys / 18 holds / 2 sells. Analyst Emmanuel Papadakis cited the mixed Q2, elevated uncertainty after sell-side meetings in London and a CEO non-deal roadshow in Frankfurt, softening prescription trends, and the removal of ziltivekimab from near-term models. [B-institutional]

Thesis read: Two-sided, but the negative side is compounding. Against it: China's NMPA accepted the Wegovy marketing application, JPMorgan raised 2026 sales estimates and its target on 25 August, and the Wegovy pill holds roughly 90% of the oral obesity market at 5m+ cumulative prescriptions. Against the position: Lilly took ~48% Q2 sales growth on Zepbound/Mounjaro, roughly 6 of 10 GLP-1 scripts are now a Lilly medicine, and Lilly's oral orforglipron (Foundayo) won its first European approval this month — a direct assault on the one segment where Novo still leads. Pulling ziltivekimab forward-out matters because it was the diversification story away from a 2027 semaglutide revenue cliff. Largest single-stock weight at 9.3%; adding here would breach the 10% ceiling.
